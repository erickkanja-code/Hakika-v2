How Hakika
actually works.

From the moment you decide to send, to the moment proof is uploaded and funds settle — in M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, or bank transfer. Every step, no jargon.

01
You decide
to send.

Open Hakika, enter the amount, pick your recipient, and choose whether this is a standard transfer or a conditional payment.

Standard transfers go straight through — fast, low fee, M-Pesa on the other end. No conditions, no escrow, no waiting. This works for when you just want to send money and you trust how it will be used.

Conditional payments are different. You add a purpose — school fees, a hospital bill, shop stock — and you define what proof you need before the money releases. The recipient knows upfront what they need to provide.

02
Money locks
in escrow.

Your payment moves onto the Stellar blockchain immediately. It is locked — visible to the recipient, but inaccessible to anyone until you release it.

This is what makes Hakika different. Your money does not sit with us. It does not sit with a bank. It sits on a public blockchain that no single party — not Hakika, not the recipient, not a government — can touch without your approval.

The recipient receives a notification the moment funds are locked. They can see the amount, the purpose, and the deadline. They know exactly what they need to do to unlock the money.

What if I change my mind? If no proof is uploaded before the deadline, the money automatically returns to you. You can also cancel an escrow early as long as no proof has been submitted yet.

03
Recipient
uploads proof.

The recipient opens their Hakika app and uploads evidence that the purpose has been fulfilled. You get a push notification the moment it lands.

Proof can be a photo, a scanned document, or a file. A school fee receipt, a hospital invoice, a photo of the goods purchased, a contractor photo of completed work. Whatever you and the recipient agreed on.

You review it on your end. Hakika does not validate the proof — you do. You know what an authentic receipt from your child's school looks like. You know what a real hospital invoice should say. That knowledge belongs with you, not an algorithm.

04
You approve.
Money moves.

One tap to review, one tap to release. Funds settle to the recipient's M-Pesa within seconds. You both get a confirmation.

The moment you release, the Stellar escrow settles instantly. The money converts and hits the recipient via their preferred local method — M-Pesa in Kenya, MTN MoMo in Nigeria, Ghana or Rwanda, Vodafone Cash in Ghana, or bank transfer. No waiting, no processing days, no bank delays.

Both of you receive a confirmation. The transaction is recorded permanently on the Stellar blockchain — a tamper-proof record that neither party can alter, ever.
