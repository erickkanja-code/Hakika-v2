How HakikaSend
actually works.

From the moment you decide to send, to the moment the proof is in and the money moves. Every step, in plain words.

01
You decide
to send.

Open the app, enter the amount, name your recipient and say what the money is for.

You choose the proof you need to see, like a fee receipt or a hospital invoice, and a deadline. Your recipient knows exactly what to send before the money moves.

02
The money
is locked.

It is held on the Stellar network the moment you confirm. Your recipient can see it is waiting for them.

The lock is a public transaction with two rules written into it. Before the deadline, only your recipient can receive it, and only when you approve. From the deadline on, only you can claim it back.

You get a link to the transaction that anyone can open and check.

Can I cancel early? No. Once it is locked, the money stays put until you release it or the deadline passes. Pick a deadline that gives them enough time.

03
Your recipient
uploads proof.

They open a private link on their phone and upload a photo or a PDF. No app, no account and no crypto wallet.

We email you the moment it lands. You review it, not us. You know what a real receipt from your child’s school looks like.

Not right? Send it back with a reason and they can upload again before the deadline.

04
You approve.
Money moves.

One tap to release. It settles on Stellar in seconds and pays out to M-Pesa.

Every step is recorded: when it was locked, when the proof came in and when it was released. You both see the same record.

In early access, payments run on test funds so you can try the whole flow first. The M-Pesa payout switches on at full launch.

Why Stellar?

Stellar is a public payment network built for moving money across borders. It settles in about five seconds and costs a fraction of a shilling per transaction.

It also has something most payment systems do not: a way to lock money with time rules written into the transaction itself. That is exactly what a payment for a purpose needs.

Your recipient never sees any of this. They see a link, an upload button and the money.

01
The deadline is enforced by the network

From the deadline on, only your account can claim the locked money. Nobody can change that rule after the lock.

02
Release happens when you approve

HakikaSend sends the release only after you tap approve. In this first version our app carries out that step for you, so you never have to handle keys.

03
Public, but private

The lock and release are visible on the public ledger. Your name, your recipient and their documents are not. Those stay in our private storage.

04
A record you can always check

Every lock, release and return has a transaction link you can open in any browser, now or years from now.
