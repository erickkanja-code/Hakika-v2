The basics
What exactly is Hakika?

Hakika is a web-based platform that lets Kenyans in the diaspora send money home with optional conditions attached. You can use it for standard transfers that land in M-Pesa fast, or for conditional transfers where the money is held in escrow until your recipient proves it will be used correctly.

The name means "certainty" in Swahili. That is the core promise — you know where your money goes.

How is this different from just sending M-Pesa?

With M-Pesa, the money lands and it is gone. You have no idea what happens to it after that. There is no condition you can attach, no proof you can require, no automatic return if it goes unspent on the right thing.

Hakika adds a trust layer on top of the existing payment rails. Standard transfers work exactly like M-Pesa. Conditional transfers let you send money with accountability built in — proof required, deadline enforced, automatic return if conditions are not met.

Do I need to download an app?

The sender's experience runs in a web browser — no app download needed. The recipient back home uses a lightweight Hakika app to receive notifications and upload proof. It is available on Android and iOS and is free to download.

Who is Hakika built for?

Hakika is built for Africans in the diaspora — primarily in the UK, US, Canada, and Gulf states — who send money home to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, or Rwanda. If you have family back home and you care how the money is used, Hakika is for you. It is designed for situations where the sender cares how the money is used, not just that it arrived.

The recipient can be any family member in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya or Rwanda with a smartphone and a mobile money account — MTN MoMo, M-Pesa, Vodafone Cash, or Airtel Money.
