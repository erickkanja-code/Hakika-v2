The basics
What is HakikaSend?

HakikaSend lets Kenyans abroad send money home for a specific purpose. The money is locked until your recipient shows the proof you asked for, like a fee receipt or a hospital invoice, and you release it.

The name comes from hakika, Swahili for certainty.

How is this different from just sending M-Pesa?

With a normal transfer, the money lands on a phone and you find out what happened only by asking. With HakikaSend you attach a purpose, a proof requirement and a deadline. Nothing moves until you have seen the proof.

Do I or my recipient need an app?

No. You use HakikaSend in your browser. Your recipient gets a private link and opens it on their phone. No download, no account and no crypto wallet for either of you.

Is early access real money?

Not yet. Early access runs on the Stellar test network with test funds, so you can try every step: lock, upload proof, release and take back. The locks and releases are real transactions on the test network. The payout to M-Pesa switches on at full launch.

Payments on proof
What counts as valid proof?

You decide when you set up the payment. A school fee receipt, a hospital invoice, a supplier invoice or photos of finished work. Your recipient uploads a photo or a PDF.

You review the proof, not HakikaSend. You know what a real receipt from your child’s school looks like.

The honest limitation: no technology can stop someone photographing a fake receipt. HakikaSend turns no accountability into documented accountability. It is not fraud-proof.

What if the proof is not right?

Send it back with a reason, like “the receipt has no school stamp”. The money stays locked and your recipient can upload again before the deadline.

What happens if the deadline passes?

If you have not released the money by the deadline, it can no longer go to the recipient. From that moment only your account can claim it back, and you do it with one tap on your payment page. That rule is part of the lock on the Stellar network.

Can I cancel before the deadline?

No. Once the money is locked it stays locked until you release it or the deadline passes. That is what makes it dependable for your recipient. Pick a deadline that gives them enough time.

Can I pay in stages, like for a build?

Yes, by making each stage its own payment. For example one for the foundation, one for the walls and one for the roof, each released on photos of that stage.

Money and fees
Which M-Pesa numbers and Paybills will work?

Payouts go to M-Pesa first, with Airtel Money planned after that. Paying a school, hospital or landlord straight to their Paybill or Till number is the next feature we are building.

What if I enter the wrong number?

Nothing is paid out until you approve the proof, so you have time to notice. Check the name on the payment page before you release.

What are the fees?

Early access is free. Before any real money moves we will publish every fee, and you will always see the fee and the exchange rate before you confirm a payment.

How long does it take?

The lock and the release each settle on Stellar in a few seconds. The rest depends on how quickly your recipient uploads proof and you review it.

Which currencies can I send from?

In early access amounts are shown in KES at an illustrative rate. We will confirm the first live send currencies before full launch. We plan to start with the currencies most Kenyans abroad earn in, like GBP, USD, EUR, CAD and AED.

Trust and security
Who holds the money while it is locked?

It sits on the Stellar network in a locked balance, not in a HakikaSend bank account. Two rules are written into that lock: before the deadline only your recipient can receive it, and from the deadline on only you can claim it back.

In this first version, the release step is carried out by our app when you tap approve, so neither of you has to manage keys. The deadline rule is enforced by the network itself.

Does my recipient need to know anything about blockchain?

No. They see a link, the amount, what you need from them and an upload button.

Is HakikaSend regulated?

Early access uses test funds only, so no real money is handled. Before real money moves we will complete the approvals needed in Kenya, including under the Virtual Asset Service Providers Act, and publish the details.

Questions? Write to erickkanja976@gmail.com and we will tell you plainly where we stand.

What happens to my data?

The lock, release and return transactions are public on the Stellar ledger, but they show only wallet addresses. Your name, contact details and uploaded documents are kept in private storage and are never published. We do not sell your data.
