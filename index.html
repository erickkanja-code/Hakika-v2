You send the money. Then what?

Every year Africans abroad send over $34 billion home to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and Rwanda combined. School fees, hospital bills, a sibling's shop stock, a house under construction. The money lands. Then you cross your fingers.

A voice note saying thanks. A blurry photo of a receipt. A call home that goes unanswered for three days. This is not how trust should work.

$34B+
sent annually to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and Rwanda, with no visibility after arrival

Hakika is not another remittance app. It is the layer that every remittance app is missing — the ability to attach a condition to your money, enforce it automatically, and know for certain that it went where you sent it.

Send freely when you want to. Send with conditions when you need to. One app handles both — with M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, and bank transfer settlement on the recipient's end, depending on their country.

The escrow sits on the Stellar blockchain. Not with Hakika. Not with a bank. Not with a family member. The conditions you set are enforced by a public ledger nobody can touch.

Four steps
to certainty.

The whole flow runs in your browser. The recipient needs the Hakika app. No bank, no agent, no middleman holding your money.

01
Send with a purpose

Enter the amount, name the recipient, state what the money is for. School fees, a hospital bill, shop stock, construction. Any purpose. Your words.

02
Funds lock in escrow

Your money moves onto the Stellar blockchain. The recipient sees it is there. Nobody can access it until you approve — including Hakika.

03
Recipient uploads proof

A fee receipt. A hospital invoice. A photo of goods purchased. They upload whatever you agreed. You get a notification the moment it arrives.

04
You release. They receive.

Review the proof, approve with one tap. Funds settle to M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, or bank transfer instantly. No proof by deadline means money returns to you. Automatically.
