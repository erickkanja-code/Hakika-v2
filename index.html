You send the money. Then what?

You send KES 45,000 for your niece’s school fees. Your sister says it is paid. Three weeks later the school calls her. It was not.

Most of what Kenyans abroad send home has a job to do. School fees, a hospital bill, stock for a shop, cement for a house. The money lands on a phone, and after that you find out only by asking.

$4.9B
sent home to Kenya by the diaspora in 2024, according to the Central Bank of Kenya

HakikaSend lets you attach a purpose to that money. You say what it is for, what proof you need and by when.

The money is locked on the Stellar network while you wait. Your recipient uploads the receipt or photos from a simple link. You look, you approve, and it moves.

If the deadline passes and you have not released it, the network lets only you take it back.

Four steps
to certainty.

You use it in your browser. Your recipient opens a link on their phone. No app to install and no wallet for anyone.

01
Send with a purpose

Enter the amount and the recipient, and say what the money is for in your own words.

02
The money is locked

It is held on Stellar the moment you confirm. Your recipient can see it is waiting for them.

03
They upload proof

A fee receipt, a hospital invoice, photos of the work. We email you as soon as it arrives.

04
You release it

Approve and it settles in seconds. Not released by the deadline? You take it back.
